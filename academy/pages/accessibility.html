Learning should be within reach.

Academy uses ordinary document navigation, readable text and flexible layouts, with keyboard and reduced-motion support.

Accessibility featuresUsing this siteKnown limits

Designed for different ways of reading.

  • A skip link moves keyboard focus to the main content.
  • Headings and landmarks describe each page’s structure.
  • Navigation links work with browser Back and Forward.
  • The mobile menu exposes its expanded state and closes with Escape.
  • Content reflows at narrow widths and browser zoom.
  • Illustrative photographs have descriptive alternative text.
  • Reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling.

Read at your own pace.

Use your browser’s zoom and text settings as needed. At a narrow width, open Menu to find the main navigation. On longer pages, “On this page” links lead to each section.

The weekly planner is a simple CSV file. The same planning guidance is available in the student hub without a download.

External resources are maintained by other organizations and may have different accessibility support. Their own help pages provide the most relevant guidance.

Scope of this statement.

This statement describes the site’s intended accessibility features; it does not claim formal certification. Browser behavior, assistive technology and external sites can differ. If a resource is difficult to use, try another resource from the library or ask your teacher or local librarian for an accessible alternative.

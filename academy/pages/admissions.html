Before you begin.

Is Morrowfield an operating school?

No. It is an original fictional academy and an educational resource demonstration. It does not offer enrollment, accreditation, school records or qualifications.

Can we use the guides at home?

Yes. The suggestions are available to read without an account. Choose materials suited to the learner, provide appropriate adult support and check the rights of external resources.

Is there an application form or a fee?

No. This site has no application process, tuition payments or donation collection. Do not send personal, financial or student details to anyone claiming to represent this fictional academy.

Where can we get help with actual coursework?

Use your real school’s established channels to contact a teacher, counselor or administrator. A local library can also help you choose suitable learning materials.

Privacy & local preferences

A plain-language description of what this site stores, and what happens when you follow a link.

On this page

No school recordsBrowser storageExternal websites & hosting

No student accounts or submissions.

This site does not provide school accounts, admissions forms, payment processing or private student records. There are no contact forms or newsletter signups on Academy pages.

The downloadable planner is a local file. Entries you add after downloading it are not sent back to this site.

Preferences stay in your browser.

The landing experience reads a session flag to remember when the main application has been unlocked in this tab. It also reads the existing timed-entry preference from local settings. It does not replace or reset other saved preferences.

The main application may store bookmarks, layout choices, settings and related preferences in local browser storage. These are browser-local and can be removed by browser cleanup, private browsing or device changes. They are not a cloud backup.

If storage is blocked, Academy guides still work and the Continue action can open the main application for that navigation. The unlock may not survive a reload.

Following a link leaves this site.

Resource links open the provider’s original website. That provider receives normal browser connection information and applies its own privacy and account policies. This page does not claim anonymity, tracking prevention or control over external services.

The host serving this site may process ordinary request information such as IP address, URL and browser details, and may keep operational logs. Academy does not add analytics or load remote fonts, scripts or images; its presentation assets are served locally.

Give an answer a sound foundation.

  1. Find the original. Trace a quotation, statistic or image to its first available source.
  2. Check authorship. Who made it, what relevant expertise do they have and what are they trying to do?
  3. Check the date and context. A sound source may still be outdated or describe a different situation.
  4. Read across sources. Look for independent evidence, not several pages repeating the same claim.
  5. Keep a source log. Record the title, author, date, link and the part you used.

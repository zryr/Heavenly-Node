Information pages & your launch choice.

Our approach, Terms of Service, Privacy, Accessibility and Credits follow the saved Search launch preferences in Settings. They open in the same tab by default. The Search new-tab choice opens a normal new tab; Search about:blank opens these local information pages inside a separate about:blank tab. Learning guides and external resource links keep ordinary browser navigation.

If your browser blocks a new tab, the selected page opens in the current tab. In an about:blank tab, Back returns through information pages or to Academy home, and Open normally opens the current document directly. Academy home, external links and Continue to Heavenly open their destination in that tab.
